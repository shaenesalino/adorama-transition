01 / Overview

Role Overview

This role supports Adorama's promotional email and SMS program, coordinating campaign intake, content, assets, segmentation, QA, launch, and performance follow-up.

Start here

Core Responsibilities

  • Create and manage email briefs, ensuring all required details (copy, assets, links, offers, and targeting) are complete and accurate.
  • Gather and validate all necessary information and assets for each campaign, including product details, pricing, URLs, and promotional messaging.
  • Coordinate with Marketing Ops, Design Team, Merchants, and other internal stakeholders to align on campaign requirements and timelines.
  • Liaise with the external agency, Alchemy Workx (AW), for email build, QA, revisions, and scheduling.
  • Build (when needed) and review email campaigns in Bluecore, ensuring correct template usage, tracking, and campaign setup.
  • Perform end-to-end QA for all campaigns, including links, pricing, personalization, dynamic content, and audience targeting.
  • Ensure alignment with campaign calendar, priorities, and business goals across email and SMS channels.
  • Manage SMS campaigns, including copywriting, segmentation recommendations, test sends, and deployment.
  • Recommend audience segmentation, exclusions, and frequency caps to optimize campaign performance.
  • Analyze campaign performance and prepare daily, weekly, and ad hoc reports, highlighting key insights and trends.
  • Identify and resolve campaign-related issues to ensure smooth execution across all sends.

A Day in the Life

  • Log in and greet the team to confirm you are online and available.
  • Prepare and send the Daily MTD and YTD report, including a summary in the team chat.
  • Attend the morning touchbase and provide updates on the status of email campaigns and other tasks.
  • Review pending tasks and continue work from the previous day.
  • Prepare 3 or more email briefs and assign them to the Internal Design Team or Alchemy Workx (AW) Designer.
  • Request SKUs and necessary product details from Merchants / Heather.
  • Coordinate with Marketing Ops, Design Team, and Alchemy Workx (AW), including AW Builder, to ensure campaigns progress smoothly from build to scheduling and deployment.
  • Create SMS copy options, send test messages, propose segments, secure approvals, and schedule SMS campaigns.
  • Identify and recommend the most appropriate audience segments for upcoming email campaigns.
  • Follow up on pending requests, approvals, and revisions to avoid delays.
  • Work on any ad hoc tasks, urgent requests, or last-minute campaign updates assigned throughout the day.
  • Monitor internal chats and emails, and respond promptly to relevant messages and updates.
  • Attend the afternoon touchbase and provide any updates or blockers.
  • Stay alert for urgent requests, last-minute changes, or New Product Announcements (NPA).

🗓 Weekly Responsibilities

  • On Mondays, prepare the Weekly Report and Summary updates, highlighting key performance insights and trends.
  • By end of day Friday, send a screenshot of all scheduled emails, ensuring campaigns are booked through Monday.

03 / Email Workflow

General Email Process

End-to-end process from briefing to post-send, ordered by when each step should begin relative to the send date. Earliest start is Step 1 (SKUs, T-10), latest is post-send. Business days count Monday–Friday only.

1

SKUs

10+ business days before

Request SKUs and complete product details from Merchants. Ensure all required information (pricing, links, images) is accurate and complete.

2

Source / UTM Codes

7+ business days before

Add the email campaigns and corresponding send times to the Source Sheet. Ensure all UTM parameters are accurate and complete.

3

Subject Line (SL), Preheader (PH) & Email Brief

7+ business days before

Create the email brief, including SL and PH. You may request the AW Copywriter to review your draft or provide recommendations for SL, PH, and email copy.

4

Jira & ClickUp Tickets

7+ business days before
  • Enter the email brief in Jira to request creative from the Internal Design Team.
  • Enter the email brief in ClickUp to request the AW Builder to build the email in Bluecore and/or request design from the AW Designer.

Note: When sending on ClickUp, ensure all additional banners (title, landing page/link, and banner asset) are included in the brief so they are incorporated into the email build. Limit to a maximum of 2 banners per email.

5

Design

3+ business days before

Ensure that the final design is received at least 3 days before the send date for review and QA.

6

Segments

2–3 business days before

Propose audience segments to Gerber and secure approval. Prepare segments as early as possible to avoid delays.

7

Build

2+ business days before

Coordinate with the AW Builder to build the email in Bluecore. Provide all final assets, links, and instructions.

Note: Test the email in Gmail to ensure proper rendering. Verify that the email is not clipped and keep the total email size at 100KB or less.

8

Routing

1–2 business days before

Request the AW Builder to route the email to Marketing, Merchants, and other stakeholders. Ensure all relevant merchants are included, especially for product-based emails.

9

Approvals

1–2 business days before

Secure final approval from Marketing Ops, Site Merchandisers, and Merchants before scheduling.

10

Scheduling

1+ business day before

Once all approvals are secured, coordinate with the AW Builder to schedule the campaign. Verify:

  • Send date and time
  • Audience segments and counts
  • UTM parameters
  • Campaign naming and setup

Note: Saturday, Sunday, and Monday emails should be scheduled on or before Friday.

11

QA — End-to-End

Continuous

Perform thorough QA across all stages:

  • Email brief (copy, links, pricing)
  • Design (layout, branding, accuracy)
  • Email proof (rendering, links, personalization)
  • Final campaign setup (segments, scheduling, tracking)
12

Post-Send Check

Day of send
  • Confirm the email was sent successfully at the scheduled time.
  • Check initial performance (clicks, revenue, engagement).
  • Flag and escalate any issues immediately.
04 / SMS

SMS Process

Seven-step process from copy creation to post-send confirmation, ordered by when each step should begin relative to the send date. Default send time is 11am local unless otherwise specified. Business days count Monday–Friday only.

Keep concise
1

Copy Creation

3+ business days before

Create 3 SMS copy options, ensuring each message fits within one SMS segment (≤160 characters).

2

Segmentation

3+ business days before

Propose the target audience segment and secure approval from Gerber.

3

Copy Review & Selection

2+ business days before

Share the copy options with the Email Team and Marketing Ops for review and selection.

4

Test Send

1+ business day before

Send a test SMS using the selected copy to validate formatting, links, and overall message clarity.

5

Link Approval

1+ business day before

Confirm with the Site Merchandiser that the landing page link is correct and approved.

Important: Always make sure the link has been converted to a short link before scheduling.

6

Scheduling

1+ business day before

Schedule the SMS campaign in Attentive, ensuring correct timing, audience, and campaign setup.

7

Post-Send Check

Day of send

Confirm the SMS was sent successfully to the expected audience at the scheduled time, and check initial performance.

08 / Design Routing

Design Guidelines

How to balance design workload between the internal Adorama Design Team and the Alchemy Workx (AW) Designer. These rules protect lead times and ensure global campaigns stay visually consistent across web and email.

Workload & SLA

  • Manage design request due dates thoughtfully.
  • Limit requests for the Adorama (ADR) Design Team to 1–2 email due dates per day.
  • Assign 3–4 emails per week to the Alchemy Workx (AW) Designer, with only 1 due per day.
  • Provide a minimum 2-day SLA for each email request.

Designer Assignment

Adorama Design Department

Internal

Assign the following:

  • Promo emails for global campaigns
  • Services: Trade, Used, EDU, Private Brands
  • New Product Announcements (NPA)
  • NPA Recap
  • Store Newsletter
  • Loyalty / VIP / PLCC campaigns

Alchemy Workx Designer

Agency

Assign the following:

  • Open Box
  • Mystery Sale
  • Flash Sale
  • Weekend Deals
  • Top Deals
  • Photo Newsletter
  • Video Newsletter
  • Events

Assignment Guidelines

  • Most email-exclusive promo campaigns are assigned to the AW Designer.
  • Global promo campaigns are primarily handled by the Adorama Design Team, as they also create corresponding web assets to ensure design consistency across channels.
  • If the Adorama Design Team is at capacity, campaigns may be assigned to the AW Designer. In such cases, provide a PSD of the email postcard or relevant web assets for reference to maintain alignment with the overall campaign design.

16 / Troubleshooting

When Things Go Wrong

Recurring issues that may come up during builds, approvals, or sends — with the playbook for each.

SKUs & Merchants

Merchant hasn’t sent SKUs by deadline
Send a same-day reminder via the email thread. If still no response, escalate to the merchant mailing list and ping Robert or the relevant category contact (Alla for Trade/Used/EDU, etc.).
Merchant sends wrong SKUs (out of stock, mispriced, wrong category)
First, confirm what’s actually live on the website using Time Machine. You can also try appending ?emailprice=t to the product URL to surface the email-specific price. If the SKU is still wrong, reach out to the merchant to confirm which price should be followed — or whether the SKU needs to be replaced.
Merchant only partially fills the SKU request
Inform Gerber of how many SKUs you received. Ask Heather / Marketing Ops to supplement the remaining SKUs.
Merchant sends SKUs after the deadline
Check whether there’s still time to update the email. If not, let the merchant know the SKU will be included in the next suitable email.

Design & Build

ADR designer is at capacity (1–2/day cap maxed)
Send the request to AW instead. If there’s an existing global asset, provide the PSD so AW can match the design system.
AW designer is at capacity (3–4/week, 1/day cap maxed)
If both ADR and AW are at full capacity, push the design due date back — making sure the new date still fits within the overall send timeline.
Design comes back wrong
If it’s a major change, ask the designer to update. If it’s a minor change you can edit yourself, do it for faster turnaround.
Email exceeds 100KB / clips in Gmail
For Newsletters, Events, Store Newsletter, and similar long-form emails: remove the PFU. For other email types: switch to the shorter Value Proposition footer, or remove it completely if needed.
HTML or email proof looks clipped
Review total email file size, simplify heavy modules, reduce unnecessary copy, and keep unsubscribe/legal content visible before the clipping point. Adding a countdown timer can also push you over — test before including one.
Pricing or savings do not match
Check the latest product feed, product page, merchant sheet, and any manual override in the build. Use Time Machine to confirm the live price for the scheduled send time.
Dynamic product block is not showing correctly
Confirm SKU eligibility, feed status, inventory availability, template conditions, and category logic.
Product feed broken / dynamic block empty on send day
Let Gerber know immediately. Reach out to Bluecore chat support and submit a support ticket right away.

Audience & Send

Email audience is lower than expected
Check frequency cap, suppression logic, segment exclusions, engagement filters, and whether Bluecore classified the campaign as lower value.
Frequency cap blocks the send
For Promo emails, override frequency capping when scheduling.

SMS

SMS short link not working
Confirm the link was converted to a short link in Attentive before scheduling. If not, the long URL eats into the character count and may break parsing.

Glossary & Acronyms

Quick reference for the abbreviations, jargon, and team names used throughout this guide.

Teams & Companies

ADRAdorama Design Team — internal design team for ADR-routed campaigns.
AWAlchemy Workx — external agency for email build, design, copywriting, QA, and scheduling. Routed via ClickUp.
PBPrivate Brands — Adorama-owned brands like Flashpoint, Godox, Glow, Clar, etc.
EDUEducation / Classroom Connect — Adorama’s education / institutional segment.

Email Cadences & Types

NPANew Product Announcement — emails sent when a brand launches a new product.
PLCCPrivate Label Credit Card — Adorama’s store credit card; campaigns about financing, statement credit, applications.
VIPLoyalty / VIP Rewards — high-value customer program emails.
BAUBusiness as Usual — recurring/regular campaign type (e.g., BAU Feature Banners ticket).
BFCMBlack Friday / Cyber Monday — the seasonal Q4 segment set used during peak holiday.

Email Components

SLSubject Line — the email’s headline shown in the inbox.
PHPreheader — the preview text shown after the subject line.
CTACall to Action — the button or link that drives the click.
SKUStock Keeping Unit — a product’s unique identifier; emails frequently feature SKUs from merchants.
UTMUrchin Tracking Module — URL parameters used to track campaign performance in analytics.

Pages & Layouts

LPLanding Page — the destination page when someone clicks through.
GLPGeneric Landing Page — the evergreen Adorama landing page (e.g., adorama.com/g/mystery-sale) reused across campaigns.

Reporting & Metrics

MTDMonth-to-Date — performance from the 1st of the month through today.
YTDYear-to-Date — performance from January 1st through today.
PCLVPredicted Customer Lifetime Value — Bluecore’s estimate of total customer worth (e.g., PCLV_25 = $25+ predicted lifetime value).
